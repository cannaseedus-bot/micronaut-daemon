Quick pointers
Start with the app brief

Describe the experience in your own words. One clear description is the main specification; the daemons can shape it into a feed, blog, news page, profile, or link hub.

Keep the first version small

Pick one main action. Add more screens after that flow works.

Public or private?

The app owner chooses visibility. Public feeds can be opened by guests; private apps require authorized access.

Subscribe to add plugins

Comments is the free first plugin. A monthly App Hub membership unlocks the paid plugin options, which are added to one selected app at a time.

Every app has its own feed

Each app ID is its data boundary: posts belong to that app and do not appear in another app’s feed. Shared sign-in can identify the same user across apps without merging app data.
